Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Welcome to Kabaka's Journal. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains what information may be collected when you visit this website, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

Kabaka's Journal is a personal publication operated by Ezeikpe Akachukwu Kingsley (Kabaka). By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

1. Information You Provide

You may choose to provide personal information when interacting with this website. For example, the contact form may ask for your name, email address and message so that you can send an enquiry.

If you choose to subscribe to the newsletter, you will be directed to a Google Forms page where you may provide information such as your email address. The information requested and submitted through that form is handled through Google's service.

Please avoid including passwords, financial information, sensitive personal details or other confidential information in ordinary contact messages.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Information you voluntarily provide may be used to:

  • Respond to questions, feedback and enquiries.
  • Communicate with people who contact the journal.
  • Manage newsletter subscriptions and send requested updates.
  • Maintain and improve the website and its services.
  • Address technical, security or administrative issues.

Your contact information is not intentionally collected through the website for sale to third parties.

3. Contact Forms and Third-Party Providers

The contact form uses Formspree to transmit submitted messages. When you submit the form, the information you enter is processed by Formspree so that the message can be delivered.

Newsletter subscriptions are handled through Google Forms. Information submitted through that form is processed by Google under the terms and privacy practices applicable to its services.

Formspree and Google operate independently of Kabaka's Journal. Their own privacy policies and terms explain how they process, store and protect information. Please review those policies before submitting information through their services.

You can learn more here:

  • Formspree Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

4. Browser Storage and Website Preferences

This website uses your browser's local storage to remember your preferred display theme, such as light or dark mode. This helps preserve your preference when you return to the website.

This preference is stored in your browser rather than being submitted to the website as a contact or newsletter record. You can clear locally stored website data through your browser settings, although doing so may reset your saved display preference.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website's current theme preference uses browser local storage. Local storage is different from a cookie. Third-party services embedded in or accessed through the website may use their own cookies or similar technologies, subject to their respective policies.

Your browser may also provide technical information when it requests website resources, such as pages, images and fonts. The hosting provider and other service providers may process technical information as part of delivering and securing their services.

6. Google Fonts

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts to display its typography. When your browser requests these fonts, Google may receive technical information associated with the request, such as your IP address and browser information.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Advertising and Future Use of Google AdSense

Kabaka's Journal may introduce third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, in the future. Advertising is not currently represented as active on this website.

If advertising is introduced, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to display advertisements, measure advertising performance, limit repeated advertisements or personalise advertising based on a visitor's interests and interactions, where permitted.

Google may use advertising cookies to serve advertisements based on a visitor's previous visits to this website or other websites. Where applicable, visitors will be able to review Google's advertising settings and available choices concerning personalised advertising.

More information is available through Google's advertising policies and technologies and Google My Ad Center.

This policy will be updated if advertising services are introduced or the website's data practices materially change.

8. External Websites and Links

Articles and pages on this website may contain links to external websites, platforms and services. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Kabaka's Journal does not control their information practices, and you should review their policies before providing personal information to them.

9. Data Retention and Security

Information submitted through third-party services may be retained by those providers according to their own retention policies and settings. Kabaka's Journal does not independently control the retention periods applied by Formspree or Google.

Reasonable care is taken when operating this website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You may choose not to submit information through the contact form or newsletter form. You can also clear your browser's locally stored website data through your browser settings.

If you have a question about information you submitted through this website, or wish to make a privacy-related request, contact the site owner using the details below. Requests concerning information held directly by Formspree or Google may need to be made to those providers.

11. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. Visitors should not submit personal information about a child through the website without the appropriate authority and consent.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the website introduces new features, changes its service providers or modifies its information practices. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.
